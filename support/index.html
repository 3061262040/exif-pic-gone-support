English

Exif Pic Gone helps you remove hidden metadata from photos before sharing. Processing happens on your device, your original image is not overwritten, and the cleaned result is exported as a new copy.

Supported Formats

  • JPG/JPEG
  • PNG
  • HEIC/HEIF

Common Questions

Does Exif Pic Gone upload my photos?

No. Photo metadata review and cleaning happen on your device. Exif Pic Gone does not upload photos to a developer-owned server.

Does the Tip Jar unlock extra features?

No. Tip Jar purchases are voluntary support for the developer. They do not unlock extra features or change the current cleaning limits.

Does Exif Pic Gone overwrite my original image?

No. The app keeps the original image unchanged and exports a cleaned copy.

Contact Support

For support requests, bug reports, or privacy questions, open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/3061262040/exif-pic-gone-support/issues

When reporting a problem, include your iOS version, the image format involved if relevant, and a short description of what you expected and what happened.

常见问题

Exif Pic Gone 会上传我的照片吗？

不会。照片元数据查看和清理都在你的设备上完成。Exif Pic Gone 不会把照片上传到开发者自有服务器。

“支持开发者”会解锁额外功能吗？

不会。“支持开发者”购买是自愿支持开发者，不会解锁额外功能，也不会改变当前清理限制。

Exif Pic Gone 会覆盖原图吗？

不会。App 会保留原图不变，并导出一个清理后的副本。
