English

Exif Pic Gone is an iOS app for reviewing and removing hidden photo metadata before sharing. This privacy policy explains how the app handles data.

Data Collection

Exif Pic Gone does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

The app does not require an account. The app does not operate a developer-owned server for photo processing, analytics, advertising, tracking, or profile building.

Photo Processing

Photo metadata review and cleaning happen on your device. Your original photo is not overwritten. When you clean a photo, the app creates a new cleaned copy for saving, exporting, or sharing.

Photos and cleaned copies are not uploaded to the developer by Exif Pic Gone.

Optional Purchases

Exif Pic Gone includes optional Tip Jar in-app purchases. Tips are voluntary and do not unlock extra features or change app limits.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase-related information under Apple's own privacy policy and App Store terms.

Permissions

Exif Pic Gone may request access to photos or files only so you can choose an image to review, clean, save, export, or share. Permission choices are controlled by iOS Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or bug reports, use the support page:

https://github.com/3061262040/exif-pic-gone-support/issues

Exif Pic Gone 是一款用于在分享前查看并移除照片隐藏元数据的 iOS App。本隐私政策说明 App 如何处理数据。

数据收集

Exif Pic Gone 不会向开发者收集、出售或共享个人数据。

App 不需要账号。App 不运行开发者自有服务器用于照片处理、分析、广告、跟踪或用户画像。

照片元数据查看和清理都在你的设备上完成。原始照片不会被覆盖。清理照片时，App 会生成一个新的干净副本，用于保存、导出或分享。

Exif Pic Gone 不会把照片或清理后的副本上传给开发者。

Exif Pic Gone 包含可选的“支持开发者”App 内购买。支持完全自愿，不会解锁额外功能，也不会改变 App 限制。

权限

Exif Pic Gone 只会在你需要选择图片进行查看、清理、保存、导出或分享时请求照片或文件访问权限。权限选择由 iOS 设置控制。
